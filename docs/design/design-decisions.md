# Design decisions

## Login page
Reference: docs/design/references/login-reference-01.png

What we like:
- Split layout: form on left, calm brand panel on right.
- Clear hierarchy: title, subtitle, form, support links.
- Minimal decoration.

What we do not want:
- Gradient mesh background.
- Glassmorphism card.
- Generic abstract 3D shapes.
- Overly large illustration.

Our version:
- Left: login form, institution logo, clean inputs.
- Right: solid deep teal panel with subtle geometric pattern, institutional name, and short tagline.
- No marketing-heavy copy.

## Dashboard
Reference: docs/design/references/dashboard-reference-01.png

What we like:
- Left sidebar, compact stat cards, clear table area.
- Strong typographic hierarchy.

Our version:
- Sidebar with primary active state.
- Four KPI cards: total employees, active employees, new employees this month, pending documents.
- Main content: recent employees table and quick actions.
- No fake charts unless real data is available.
